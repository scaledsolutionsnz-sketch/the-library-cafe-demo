What comes over the counter

Order at the counter, grab a table, and we bring it out. Everything made to have alongside a good book or a better yarn.

Coffee

Espresso, made properly

Flat whites, long blacks and mochas pulled with care. One regular calls it the best hot coffee in most of Levin.

Cabinet

Fresh cabinet food

Sandwiches, savouries and baking filled each morning. Made for morning tea runs and quick lunches between errands.

Hot lunch

Roast meals and hot lunches

A proper cooked lunch without cooking it yourself. Regulars rate the roast meals for value on a cold Horowhenua day.

Sweet

Cakes and slices

Something sweet to go with the coffee. Cakes, slices and biscuits from the cabinet, easy to share and easy to finish.

Tea and more

Tea, hot chocolate and fluffies

Pots of tea, rich hot chocolate and a fluffy for the little ones while you get ten minutes to yourself.

Space

Room for a catch-up

Tables with space for prams, study groups and long conversations, right inside Levin's library and community hub.

Bath Street, Levin

The cafe in the middle of everything

The Library Cafe sits in the heart of Te Takeretanga o Kura-hau-po, Levin's library and community hub. Call in after story time, between errands, or while the kids pick out their books.

Order at the counter, find a seat, and your coffee comes to you. Parking is close, the town centre is at the door, and nobody minds if you stay a while.

  • Inside Te Takere at 10 Bath Street
  • Open every weekday until 3pm
  • Counter service, no bookings needed
