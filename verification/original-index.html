Bath Street, Levin

The cafe in the middle of everything

The Library Cafe sits in the heart of Te Takeretanga o Kura-hau-po, Levin's library and community hub. Call in after story time, between errands, or while the kids pick out their books.

Order at the counter, find a seat, and your coffee comes to you. Parking is close, the town centre is at the door, and nobody minds if you stay a while.

  • Inside Te Takere at 10 Bath Street
  • Open every weekday until 3pm
  • Counter service, no bookings needed
