Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

This website does not use contact forms and does not collect personal information directly. If you phone or email us, we only use your details to respond to you.

2. Third-party services

This website embeds a Google Map and links to Google, Facebook and Instagram. Those services may collect usage data under their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies if you have concerns.

3. Cookies

We do not set our own cookies. Embedded third-party content such as Google Maps may set cookies of its own.

4. Your information

Any details you share with us by phone or email are kept only as long as needed to help you, and are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
